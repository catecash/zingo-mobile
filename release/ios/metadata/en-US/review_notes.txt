App description
Catecash is a self-custody iPhone and iPad wallet for CATE on the Catecash network. CATE holders need a mobile way to manage funds and verify transactions while retaining control of their recovery phrase. The app creates or restores a wallet on the device, shows balances and transaction history, displays receiving addresses and QR codes, and connects to a Catecash wallet server for chain data and transaction submission.

Instructions
1. Open the app and tap Create new wallet. Follow the recovery phrase backup flow. No account, login, or demo credentials are required. Use a new review wallet rather than a personal recovery phrase.
2. After the wallet opens, allow it to sync. The Home screen shows the balance and sync status. Tap Receive to view an address and QR code. Tap History to view transactions. A new empty wallet has no transaction history.
3. Tap Options, then Server, to inspect the Catecash Mainnet connection. The default server is https://wallet.catecash.com:443. The reviewer can choose a custom server.
4. Tap Send to enter a Catecash address or scan its QR code. The reviewer can inspect the Send form with a new wallet. A completed transfer requires a funded wallet and destination address.
5. To test recovery, use Restore wallet on the opening screen with the review wallet's recovery phrase, then allow the wallet to sync.

Protected resources
The camera scans QR codes for wallet addresses, recovery phrases, and viewing keys when the reviewer opens a scanner. To test it, open Send and tap the scan button beside the destination address. Face ID unlocks the wallet when the reviewer enables biometric lock in Options. The camera SDK contains optional location APIs, but Catecash disables location support and does not request location for QR scanning. The Info.plist includes NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription because the linked SDK references Core Location.

External services
The default Catecash wallet endpoint, wallet.catecash.com, provides blockchain data and accepts signed transactions through the Catecash light-wallet service. Outgoing transactions use the Nym mixnet transport. Optional support and privacy links open catecash.com. Face ID uses Apple's on-device authentication. The app has no account authentication provider, AI model, fiat payment processor, or in-app purchase service.

Regional differences
The app is offered in the United States only. Its wallet features work the same throughout the United States, and it has no location-based feature switch. Network access to the Catecash endpoint and Nym is required for sync and sending.
