CATE holders need a mobile way to manage funds and verify transactions while retaining control of their recovery phrase. Catecash provides a self-custody wallet for iPhone and iPad. Create a wallet on your device, or restore one with a recovery phrase.

Receive CATE with a wallet address or QR code. Send CATE by entering an address or scanning a QR code. Review your balance and transaction history as the wallet syncs with the Catecash network.

View balances by pool, inspect sync progress, and manage your receiving addresses and saved contacts. Use the default Catecash wallet server or choose a custom server.

No account is required. Write down your recovery phrase and keep it safe. Catecash cannot recover it for you.
